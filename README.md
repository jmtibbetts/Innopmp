# Innopmp: PMP® Exam Study Kit

A study kit for the PMP® certification exam, aligned to PMI's PMP® Examination Content Outline (July 2026). It has three companion resources: a slide deck for structured review, an interactive study guide for practice, and a printable quick reference for last-minute revision.

## What's included

| File | Format | Use it for |
|------|--------|------------|
| `PMP_Exam_Study_Deck.pptx` | PowerPoint, 129 slides | Working through the content outline domain by domain, with frameworks, worked examples, and exam cues |
| `PMP_Study_Guide.html` | Interactive web page | Self-study with progress tracking, flashcards, a scenario quiz, and an earned value calculator |
| `PMP_Quick_Reference.pdf` | 3-page PDF | A printable summary of key takeaways, domains, and every formula |

## How the exam is built

- **180 questions in 240 minutes:** 170 scored and 10 unscored pretest items, split into a case-study section and two parts of independent questions, with two 10-minute breaks.
- **Three domains, 26 tasks:** People 33% (8 tasks), Process 41% (10 tasks), and Business Environment 26% (8 tasks).
- **Mixed approaches:** about 40% predictive and 60% agile or hybrid questions, spread across every domain.

## Topics covered

**People:** situational leadership, Tuckman's stages and the team charter, psychological safety, RACI, Maslow, McGregor, Herzberg, levels of conflict, stakeholder analysis (power/interest grid, salience model, engagement matrix), communication channels, and knowledge transfer.

**Process:** the project charter and integrated plan, scope and the WBS, precedence relationships, critical path, forward and backward pass, total and free float, resource leveling and smoothing, quality tools (fishbone, control charts, the rule of seven, cost of quality), contracts and procurement, performance measurement, project artifacts, and project closure.

**Business Environment:** OPAs and EEFs, organizational structures, governance and the PMO, the PMI Code of Ethics, compliance, integrated change control and change request types, risk management, risks versus issues, impediments, organizational change (Lewin, Kotter, ADKAR), PESTLE, AI in project management, and sustainability.

**Agile essentials:** the Agile Manifesto, life cycles and choosing an approach, servant leadership, Scrum (events, artifacts, pillars, and values), Kanban and lean (jidoka and kaizen), story mapping, relative estimation, agile metrics, agile contracts, and scaling frameworks.

**Budget and earned value:** estimate accuracy (ROM to definitive), three-point and PERT estimating, building the cost baseline and reserves, earned value measures, forecasting (EAC, ETC, VAC, TCPI), and a one-page formula review.

## Using the interactive study guide

Open `PMP_Study_Guide.html` in any modern browser. It works offline and needs no installation.

- **Study guide:** searchable topics with exam cues and sources. Mark each topic as studied to track your progress.
- **Flashcards:** flip through key terms, filtered by section or shuffled.
- **Quiz:** exam-style scenario questions, each with an explanation.
- **EVM calculator:** enter BAC, PV, EV, and AC to see every earned value measure and a plain-language reading.

Progress and theme settings are saved in your browser only.
